Your privacy, in plain words

Your details are visible to only the companies your counsellor confirms with you first. Nothing about other candidates ever appears on your page, and your team’s internal notes are never shown here — only updates written for you.

  • Sharing consent — given 5 Nov 2026, for Northgate Systems, Data Analyst role only.
  • Future roles — you have not opted in to a general talent pool; each new company needs your fresh OK.
  • Updates — you receive status and interview updates on WhatsApp and email (headline + link only, never the full details in the message). Recommendations arrive separately, and only if you ask for them.
  • Your controls — visibility, blocked companies and notification choices live in Your choices above; you can also download your data or ask for it to be deleted. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it was.
